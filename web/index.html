Content

Images are scaled to the full 384-dot width. You can also paste an image.

Starts with a staircase ruler: each step is one line lower, and the numbers count lines from the start of the job. The first number you can see on paper tells you how many lines the printer dropped. The test pattern always prints with no lead-in and no trimming.

Rendering

Thermal dots spread, so dithered images print darker than their dot pattern. Dot gain lightens images before dithering to compensate. 1 turns it off. It only affects dither mode.

Preview

Print

The paper motor needs about 16 lines to get up to speed, and anything printed before that comes out squashed.

8 lines = 1 mm. The tear edge is about 70 lines past the print head, so less than that leaves the end of the print inside.

If prints come out cut short, raise the packet gap. Keep this tab in the foreground while printing.
